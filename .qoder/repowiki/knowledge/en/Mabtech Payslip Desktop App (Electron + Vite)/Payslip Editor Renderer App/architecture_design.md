The app is a single-page Electron renderer rooted at `main.tsx` → `App.tsx`. `App` owns the only source of truth — a `Db` object (sheets + settings) loaded via `lib/store` and mutated through an exclusive `mutate()` callback that debounces writes to disk. UI components (`SheetTable`, `SettingsModal`, `Preview`) are pure presentational children; they receive data and callbacks from `App` rather than accessing store directly. Cross-process operations (file open/save, PDF rendering, backup/restore, logo fetch) go through the injected `window.api` IPC bridge, keeping Electron-specific calls out of the component tree. Rendering utilities in `lib/payslipHtml` and `lib/payslipDocx` consume the same `SalaryRow` + `settings` shape produced by `importSheet`/`store`, so import, edit, and export all share one data contract defined in `types.ts`.
