The app is a single-page Electron renderer rooted at `main.tsx` → `App.tsx`. `App` owns the only source of truth — a `Db` (sheets + settings) loaded via `lib/store` and mutated exclusively through a debounced `mutate()` callback. UI components (`SheetTable`, `SettingsModal`, `AdvanceEditor`, `Preview`) are pure presentational children receiving data and callbacks from `App`; they never touch store or IPC directly. Cross-process operations go through the injected `window.api` bridge. `SheetTable` renders inline number inputs plus a ≡ list button for the three dated fields (`bankAdvance`, `cashAdvance`, `bankSalary`) and delegates editing to `App` via an `onEditAdvances` callback; `App` opens the `AdvanceEditor` modal seeded with either the stored dated items or the legacy flat amount, then writes back both the item list (`advanceItemsKey(field)`) and its summed total (`advanceItemsTotal`). Rendering utilities in `lib/payslipHtml` and `lib/payslipDocx` consume the same `SalaryRow` + `settings` shape produced by `importSheet`/`store`, so import, edit, and export all share one data contract defined in `types.ts`.
