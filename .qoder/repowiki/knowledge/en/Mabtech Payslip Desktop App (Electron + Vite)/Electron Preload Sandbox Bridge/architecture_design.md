Single-entry preload module with three parallel files serving different build/typing roles: `index.js` is the runtime preload entry (bundled by Electron), `index.ts` is the TypeScript source used for type inference, and `index.d.ts` declares the public `PayslipApi` shape plus augments `Window.api` so the renderer can call `window.api.*` with full types. All methods are thin wrappers around `ipcRenderer.invoke(channel, payload)`, grouping channels by domain (`db:*`, `app:*`, `file:*`, `payslip:*`). The bridge boundary is enforced through `contextBridge.exposeInMainWorld('api', api)` — the renderer never imports Electron modules directly.
