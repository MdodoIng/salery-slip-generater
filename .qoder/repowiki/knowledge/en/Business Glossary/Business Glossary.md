### Mabtech Payslip Generator
- Definition：The internal name of this single-user, single-company desktop tool used by Mabtech Equipment Pvt. Ltd. to convert a monthly Excel salary register into one branded A4 payslip per employee.
- Aliases：Mabtech Payslip、Payslip Generator

### Salary month
- Definition：A payroll period keyed as `YYYY-MM`; each month's data lives on its own sheet in the source Excel file and is stored separately in the local database.

### HRA
- Definition：House Rent Allowance — a new allowance column added to the salary sheet template that needs to flow through the importer, type model, and payslip templates.
- Aliases：House Rent Allowance

### PF
- Definition：Provident Fund — a new deduction component added to the salary sheet template alongside HRA and ESI.
- Aliases：Provident Fund

### ESI
- Definition：Employee State Insurance — a new deduction component added to the salary sheet template alongside HRA and PF.
- Aliases：Employee State Insurance

### Authorised Signatory
- Definition：A free-form field in Settings that is printed on the payslip as a signature label; it is not an authentication mechanism.
